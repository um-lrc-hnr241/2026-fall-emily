The meaning behind our theme · Westworld

Where certainty shatters.

Inspired by Emily's story in Westworld, Frostline explores the fragile boundaries between reality and illusion, humanity and artificial consciousness, and certainty and doubt.

Emily's relationship with her father, William, is marked by emotional distance and distrust. When William becomes convinced that Emily is a host rather than his daughter, his certainty leads to tragedy. His failure to recognize her shows how devastating our assumptions about another person's identity can become.

Ice represents that fragile certainty: a surface that seems solid until a crack reveals what lies beneath. Our shattered-ice design reflects the philosophical questions we explore through Hegel, Fanon, and Westworld—how we recognize others, how we know ourselves, and what happens when the truths we depend on break apart.
